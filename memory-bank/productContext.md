# Product Context

## Problem Statement
Real-time tracking and inventory management across diverse devices and field conditions require zero-latency updates, offline reliability, and an intuitive, glanceable interface.

## User Experience Goals
- Adhere strictly to Apple Human Interface Guidelines (HIG) for a clean, deferential layout.
- Ensure zero-latency UI updates during active navigation and stock adjustments.
- Provide high-contrast, easily tappable interfaces (minimum 44x44pt) for fast-paced mobile and kitchen environments.
